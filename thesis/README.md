# From Correctness to the Quantum Oracle: Exact Modular Rounding, Structured Cryptanalysis, and Conditional Security of C1-K4

Samir Bouftass · Version 0.6 · English.

This twelve-chapter independent research manuscript develops exact quotient and remainder arithmetic for C1-K4-P12-CBD1. It derives two-sided decryption-failure bounds, relates passive security to two specified CBD1 Module-LWE distributions, and studies conditional Fujisaki–Okamoto reductions in classical and quantum random-oracle experiments. It documents the provenance of lattice attack estimates and establishes a same-tape, two-key second-moment bound. Concrete source hardness, a transfer for the complete non-generic failure game, and a 128-bit post-quantum security claim remain open.
