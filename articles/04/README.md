# Find-Failing-Plaintext Analysis of C1-K4-P12-CBD1

Samir Bouftass · Version 4.4 · English.

For independent keys with shared encryption randomness, the paper bounds the second moment of the failure probability by 2^-247 and the associated standard deviation by 2^-123.5. It distinguishes oracle-selected tapes from freely chosen tapes and analyzes the resulting failure-finding experiments. Fixed-tape controls accompany the finite certificates. Applicability of sharper FFP-NG results to the compressed and lifted C1-K4 construction remains open.
