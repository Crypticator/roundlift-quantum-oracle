# A Quotient–Remainder Calculus for Modular Rounding: Exact Carries, Distributional Laws, Nested Truncation, and the Boundary with Learning With Rounding

Samir Bouftass · Version 4.3 · English.

For a modulus divisible by the cell width, the paper represents each residue by its quotient and remainder. It derives arithmetic identities for carries and nested truncations, the conditional law of randomized lifting, and entropy bounds. It identifies the exact scalar agreement with the floor-rounding convention of Learning With Rounding and explains the additional distributions needed to compare structured cryptographic problems.
